**Companion documents.** The *MRFS Overview* introduces the standard for a general reader. The *MRFS Validation Report* reports how the reference implementation behaves on synthetic data, including its limitations. Neither is normative.

**Endorsement.** This document refers to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and to the four-fifths rule of the U.S. Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures to explain how MRFS relates to them. MRFS is not affiliated with or endorsed by NIST or other referenced organizations.

**Version compatibility.** E1 results from MRFS 2.0.0 are not directly comparable with those from MRFS 1.0 or 1.1. See Appendix B.
